How this is calculated

swing = 2026 margin − 2024 baseline margin

  • Margin is the Democratic share minus the Republican share, using shares of all votes cast, not the two-party vote. A positive swing is toward Democrats; a negative swing is toward Republicans. We show both the same way.
  • Baseline. Counties use the 2024 presidential result. Districts use the 2024 House result, and only where the 2024 and 2026 lines are the same. In redistricted states, only counties are compared.
  • Unopposed races have no two-sided margin. Their places are excluded and the count of exclusions is shown.
  • Boundaries changed; not comparable. A place whose FIPS code does not exist in both years is listed but not scored. Connecticut's planning regions are the main case.
  • Alaska reports by state house district, not county, and is shown that way.
  • Uncertified counts are labelled until the state certifies. The swing may change by a tenth of a point when it does.

Method version 1.0 · Characteristics: Census Bureau, American Community Survey 2020 to 2024 five-year estimates.
