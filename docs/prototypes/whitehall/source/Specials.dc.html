How the index is calculated

overperformance = special margin − 2024 presidential margin in the same district

  • The index is the unweighted mean of overperformance across all contested specials since Jan 1, 2027. A state house seat counts the same as a U.S. House seat.
  • Uncontested specials have no two-sided margin. They are listed and left out of the index.
  • No baseline. Where 2024 presidential results by district are not available, the special is listed with "No baseline" and left out. It is not dropped silently.
  • Runoffs and first rounds. An all-party first round is scored by the combined vote of each party's candidates. A runoff is a separate contest.
  • Both directions are written the same way: points toward Democrats, or points toward Republicans.
