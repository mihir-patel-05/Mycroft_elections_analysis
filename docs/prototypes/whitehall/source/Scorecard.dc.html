How the score works

Brier = average of (probability given − outcome)², where outcome is 1 if it happened and 0 if not

  • Lower is better. Saying 90% for a winner scores 0.01. Saying 90% for a loser scores 0.81. Always saying 50% scores 0.25.
  • Cut-off. A signal is scored on its last value at or before 6:00 PM ET on Nov 3, 2026. A race with no value by then is not scored for that signal. It is never counted as a 0.
  • Types. A market price is read as a probability (71¢ is 71%). A poll average gives a margin, not a probability, so it has no Brier score; it is scored on races called right and on average error in points.
  • Provisional. Races not yet certified are scored on the locked count and marked provisional. Scores are final when all 470 are certified.
  • Runoffs and ranked choice. A runoff is scored on the runoff. Ranked-choice races are scored on the final round.
  • Race ratings were not archived for 2026, so they cannot be scored. The daily archive that began on Nov 30, 2026 fixes this for later elections.

Method version 1.0. These are other people's signals. None of them is Whitehall's forecast.
